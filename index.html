24-HR SCHEDULE
The timeline of the verse
Day 1 — 08:30 AM - 10:00 AM

Registrations & ID Card Distribution

Teams arrive, register on-site, collect kits, and get ready to enter the CypherVerse.

Day 1 — 10:30 AM - 11:30 AM

Inauguration & Opening Ceremony

Inauguration ceremony at the Auditorium with rules briefing, evaluation guidelines, and mentor introductions.

Day 1 — 12:00 PM

Hacking Begins

Reporting to designated labs. The 24-hour hackathon timer officially starts ticking!

Day 1 — 02:00 PM

Lunch Break

Delicious meal served for all participants and mentors to keep the energy flowing.

Day 1 — 04:00 PM

Mentor Review Round 1

Mentors visit tables for initial architectural feedback, feasibility checks, and technical guidance.

Day 1 — 05:00 PM

Snacks & High Tea

Quick refreshment break with snacks, tea, and coffee to power up the evening sprints.

Day 1 — 08:00 PM

Dinner

Nutritious dinner served for all participants before the night-long hacking phase.

Day 1 — 11:00 PM - 12:00 AM

Ice Breaking & Mini Games

Chill time with fun games, music, and community activities to refresh your mind.

Day 2 — 12:00 AM

Midnight Check-In

Midnight checkpoint — verification of repository milestones in respective labs.

Day 2 — 07:00 AM - 08:30 AM

Mentor Review Round 2

Final round of mentorship and pitch rehearsal before the hard code freeze.

Day 2 — 08:30 AM

Final Submission Deadline

Hard code freeze! Submit github repos, demo links, and video pitches on Unstop.

Day 2 — 09:00 AM - 11:00 AM

Breakfast

Morning breakfast to recharge for the judging evaluations and presentations.

Day 2 — 11:00 AM - 02:00 PM

Project Demonstrations & Q/A

Teams showcase live working prototypes to the jury panel with interactive Q&A.

Day 2 — 02:00 PM

Results & Award Ceremony

Grand prize distribution, certificate presentation, and closing photo ceremony!
